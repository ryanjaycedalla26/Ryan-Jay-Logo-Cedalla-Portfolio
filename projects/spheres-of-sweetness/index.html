Illustration · PROJECT 01

Spheres of Sweetness

Completed 2026 Graphic Designer Graphic Design

Spheres of Sweetness is a colorful fruit illustration that showcases a combination of oranges, grapes, blueberries, and fresh green leaves. The artwork highlights the beauty of nature through vibrant colors, detailed textures, and overlapping fruit shapes, creating a visually appealing and balanced composition.

Project Overview

This project aims to demonstrate the effective application of the seven elements of design and principles of design through a hand-drawn fruit composition. Using colored pencils, different fruits are illustrated with attention to their shapes, textures, colors, and forms. The arrangement creates a harmonious visual presentation while highlighting creativity, artistic expression, and fundamental graphic design concepts.

Design Challenge

Combining different fruits, colors, and textures into one composition while maintaining visual balance and harmony can be challenging.

Approach

The artwork uses proper color combinations, shading, overlapping shapes, and textures to create a balanced and visually appealing fruit illustration.

Project Highlights

  • Fruit Variety: Includes oranges, grapes, and blueberries.
  • Vibrant Colors: Uses a combination of warm and cool colors.
  • Detailed Textures: Shows different fruit surfaces through dots and shading.
  • 3D Effects: Uses shading to add depth and dimension.
  • Balanced Composition: Arranges fruits harmoniously.
  • Visual Emphasis: Highlights the fruits through their size and colors.
