Typography · PROJECT 04

Anchor — Typography Poster

Completed 2026 Graphic Designer Graphic Design

ANCHOR is a volleyball apparel brand that represents strength, confidence, and dedication. Its bold typography and minimalist design reflect the competitive spirit of volleyball.

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a typography-based brand identity poster for ANCHOR, a fictional volleyball apparel brand. The design highlights the brand's identity through bold lettering, strong visual hierarchy, and a black, white, and red color palette.

Design Challenge

Creating a recognizable brand identity using typography alone while maintaining visual impact and clarity.

Approach

Used bold typography, contrasting colors, and organized layouts to create a strong and professional brand identity.

Project Highlights

  • Bold Typography: Uses heavy fonts to emphasize the brand name and message.
  • Minimalist Design: Focuses on text and geometric elements.
  • Color Contrast: Combines black, white, and red for a striking appearance.
  • Strong Branding: Features the ANCHOR name prominently.
  • Motivational Slogan: "Stay Low. Stay Ready."
  • Volleyball Identity: Incorporates volleyball-related terms and defensive concepts.
  • Brand Message: Promotes strength, readiness, and dedication.
